Our Heritage & Philosophy

Crafting Culinary Poetry With Every Pinch of Spice

Founded with a passion to revive the forgotten culinary opulence of Awadh, Punjab, and the coastal backwaters of Kerala, The Spice Garden is more than a restaurant — it is an unforgettable feast for the senses.

Our master chefs spend hours hand-roasting whole spices, slowly simmering rich satin gravies in heavy brass handis, and baking soft, blistered naans against glowing clay tandoor walls. We never compromise on authenticity, blending time-honored royal recipes with contemporary plating artistry.

Single-Origin Spices

Cardamom from Idukki, saffron from Kashmir, and stone-ground turmeric for pure aroma.
